# Emerald completion rules

- Play as Dark with the male avatar. Never give a nickname to any Pokemon. When asked for one, choose NO and verify the prompt before confirming.
- Aim to unlock the complete Emerald achievement set. Before a one-time battle, item, encounter, gym leader, story transition, legendary battle, or Trick House exit, check the current checkpoint and save in game.
- Make an in-game save at least every 30 minutes of real time. The goal includes the age of the last confirmed save. After the game visibly confirms a save, choose SAVE_CONFIRMED so the harness records its time.
- Keep a recoverable in-game save before missable events. If a missable is lost, stop and reload the most recent save that predates it. Never start a new game to recover a missable.
- Do not cross a checkpoint boundary until its listed achievements and Scott encounters are verified. If evidence is unclear, choose NEED_USER_INPUT for review rather than guessing.
- The current checkpoint below is a short slice of the full checklist in instructions/emerald-missables.md. Follow it before moving to the next area.
