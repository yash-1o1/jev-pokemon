# Emerald missable checkpoint ledger

The [RetroAchievements Emerald set](https://retroachievements.org/game/668) currently marks 28 achievements as missable. This working ledger collects the route constraints identified in the [community guide](https://github.com/RetroAchievements/guides/wiki/(WIP)-Pokemon-Emerald-(Game-Boy-Advance)), including Scott's 13 meetings. The guide is a work in progress, so cross-check the live missable filter before each story boundary. Keep completed evidence in the ignored `.local/ra-run` folder.

## Early Hoenn

- [ ] Rescue Birch for Zigzagone; get the free Oldale Potion for Loyalty Program; win the Route 103 rival battle.
- [ ] Meet Scott after Wally's catching tutorial in Petalburg (1/13).
- [ ] Get Wailmer Pail from the Pretty Petal flower shop on Route 104.
- [ ] In Rustboro, talk to Scott at Trainer School before Roxanne (2/13), receive Quick Claw, and defeat every gym trainer before Roxanne.
- [ ] Talk to Scott again at Trainer School after Roxanne and before receiving Devon Goods (3/13).
- [ ] Take the optional rival battle at Rustboro City or Route 104.
- [ ] Defeat every Dewford gym trainer before Brawly.
- [ ] Meet Scott after the Slateport museum (4/13), then at the Slateport Battle Tent before leaving (5/13).
- [ ] Clear every trainer and item in each Trick House puzzle before completing that puzzle; the old rooms cannot be revisited.
- [ ] Defeat every Mauville gym trainer before Wattson. Meet Scott after Wally (6/13).
- [ ] Meet Scott inside Verdanturf Battle Tent after Wattson and before Fiery Path (7/13).

## Midgame

- [ ] Meet Scott inside Fallarbor Battle Tent before Team Magma takes the Meteorite (8/13).
- [ ] Defeat the suspicious Magma grunt on Jagged Pass before the story removes him.
- [ ] Defeat every Lavaridge gym trainer before Flannery.
- [ ] Defeat every Petalburg gym trainer before Norman.
- [ ] Defeat every Weather Institute grunt before clearing the Institute; meet Scott after the Route 119 rival battle (9/13).
- [ ] Defeat every Fortree gym trainer before Winona; receive Scott's call after Winona (10/13).
- [ ] Defeat the Lilycove rival before they disappear.
- [ ] Meet Scott upstairs in the Lilycove motel before Team Aqua steals the submarine (11/13).
- [ ] Defeat every Magma Hideout grunt before Maxie; collect its items.
- [ ] Defeat every Aqua Hideout grunt before Archie in Seafloor Cavern; collect its items.
- [ ] Meet Scott outside Mossdeep Space Center before defeating Team Magma (12/13).
- [ ] Defeat every Mossdeep gym trainer before Tate and Liza.
- [ ] Defeat every Seafloor Cavern grunt before Archie; collect its items.

## Late game and one-time catches

- [ ] Save before each Regi and Rayquaza battle; catch each without permanently knocking it out.
- [ ] Meet Scott at the Ever Grande Pokemon Center before Victory Road and before the League (13/13) to complete Teacher's Pet.
- [ ] Save before choosing Latias or Latios after the League, and before the roaming catch; catch one without permanently knocking it out.
- [ ] Keep the Dig TM and Sea Incense for later completion goals.

Before any listed boundary, save in game and retain a backup of the preceding save. If a checkpoint fails, reload that save; do not start a new game.
