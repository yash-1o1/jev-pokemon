# Emerald missable checkpoint ledger

The live RetroAchievements base set marks 28 achievements as missable. This full ledger is included in **every** Emerald achievement decision. The achievement model must check it before moving through a story boundary, clearing a one-time area, or finishing a Trick House puzzle. The guide is community maintained and may lag set revisions, so verify current in-game state and the live achievement list when uncertain.

## Chronological missables

- [ ] **Battle Addict - Rustboro:** defeat every Rustboro Gym trainer before defeating Roxanne.
- [ ] **How About a Little Battle?:** defeat the Rustboro/Route 104 rival while this optional battle is available; do not let story progression remove it.
- [ ] **Battle Addict - Dewford:** defeat every Dewford Gym trainer before defeating Brawly.
- [ ] **Battle Addict - Mauville:** defeat every Mauville Gym trainer before defeating Wattson.
- [ ] **Amoonguss Would Debut 6 Years Later:** defeat the suspicious Team Magma grunt on Jagged Pass before the Mt. Chimney story removes him.
- [ ] **Battle Addict - Lavaridge:** defeat every Lavaridge Gym trainer before defeating Flannery.
- [ ] **Battle Addict - Petalburg:** defeat every Petalburg Gym trainer before defeating Norman.
- [ ] **Panic! At the Institute:** defeat every Team Aqua grunt in the Weather Institute before finishing its story event.
- [ ] **Battle Addict - Fortree:** defeat every Fortree Gym trainer before defeating Winona.
- [ ] **I'll Battle with You:** defeat the Lilycove rival before the submarine story sequence makes the battle unavailable.
- [ ] **Earth Exterminator:** defeat every grunt and collect every item in the Magma Hideout before completing it.
- [ ] **Valiant Vigilante:** defeat every grunt and collect every item in the Aqua Hideout before completing it.
- [ ] **Battle Addict - Mossdeep:** defeat every Mossdeep Gym trainer before defeating Tate and Liza.
- [ ] **Not the Space, Not the Time:** defeat every Team Magma grunt in the Mossdeep Space Center before the story battle clears the area.
- [ ] **Ocean Obstructor:** defeat every grunt and collect every item in Seafloor Cavern before defeating Archie.
- [ ] **Battle Addict - Sootopolis:** defeat every Sootopolis Gym trainer before defeating Juan.
- [ ] **Teacher's Pet:** meet Scott on all 13 occasions. Track each separately: (1) Petalburg after Wally's catching tutorial; (2) Rustboro Trainer School before Roxanne; (3) Rustboro Trainer School after Roxanne and before receiving Devon Goods; (4) after the Slateport museum battle; (5) at the Slateport Battle Tent after the museum and before leaving; (6) after Wally at Mauville Gym; (7) Verdanturf Battle Tent after Wattson and before Fiery Path; (8) Fallarbor Battle Tent before Team Magma takes the Meteorite; (9) Route 119 after the rival battle; (10) Scott's call after Winona; (11) Lilycove motel before Team Aqua steals the submarine; (12) Mossdeep Space Center before defeating Team Magma; (13) Ever Grande Pokémon Center before Victory Road/the Pokémon League. Several are explicitly missable; confirm each in the checkpoint before crossing its deadline.
- [ ] **Reward by Reputation:** claim Scott's 4 BP Battle Frontier reward when offered; do not leave the encounter unresolved.
- [ ] **It Moved!:** save before the one-time Sudowoodo encounter at the Battle Frontier and catch it. Do not knock it out or flee.
- [ ] **Gotta Catch 'Em All:** obtain all 212 Pokémon available in Emerald. Maintain a separate local species ledger; preserve unique gifts, fossils, the chosen starter, one-time encounters, and trade candidates until the achievement is confirmed. The guide warns this achievement can become unobtainable if a species not obtainable in Emerald or a second starter enters the save. Dark chose Mudkip: never choose Treecko or Torchic, never import Pokémon from another save, and verify a species is in Emerald's obtainable set before catching or trading for it. Do not release a unique species before checking the live set's requirement.

## Trick House puzzles

Each puzzle becomes unavailable after it is completed. Before taking the scroll to the Trick Master or leaving the room, search the whole puzzle, defeat every trainer, and collect its item. Verify the achievement unlock before starting the next puzzle.

- [ ] Puzzle 1 — **Trick Master Is Fabulous** (all trainers and items).
- [ ] Puzzle 2 — **Trick Master Is Smart** (all trainers and items).
- [ ] Puzzle 3 — **Trick Master Is Coveted** (all trainers and items).
- [ ] Puzzle 4 — **Trick Master Is Cool** (all trainers and items).
- [ ] Puzzle 6 — **Trick Master Is My Life** (all trainers and items).
- [ ] Puzzle 7 — **Trick Master Is Huggable** (all trainers and items).
- [ ] Puzzle 8 — **Trick Master I Love** (all trainers and items).
- [ ] After the final puzzle, inspect behind the Trick Master and collect the hidden Nugget for **Nugget of Wisdom** before leaving.

## Before every boundary

1. Read this complete list and the current local checkpoint. Check the RetroArch achievements page for the current unlock state.
2. Save in game before a missable event and retain a backup of the preceding save.
3. Do not proceed through a deadline until the relevant battle, item sweep, Scott meeting, or capture is visibly verified and its achievement unlock is confirmed where applicable.
4. If something may have been missed, stop and reload the latest save from before that event. Never restart from the beginning.
5. Save at least every 30 minutes and before a missable event. Never nickname a Pokémon.
